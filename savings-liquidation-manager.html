How it reads: health factor and totals come straight from the Aave Pool contract; per-asset balances, liquidation thresholds and prices come from Aave's data provider and price oracle. A liquidation price is the price at which that one asset alone would push health factor to 1.0, with everything else held at today's price. The stress slider moves all non-stablecoin assets down together, which is closer to how a real crash works. Only assets that have a collateral factor on Aave count toward liquidation, and that includes tokens like ARB, OP and AAVE on the networks where Aave lists them. Assets you supplied that have no collateral factor are shown in a separate table. On Ethereum this reads the main Aave V3 market. Not financial advice.
